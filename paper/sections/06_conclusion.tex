\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented VulGCL, a multimodal vulnerability detection framework that
jointly fuses graph (GNN), visual (CNN), and semantic (CodeBERT) representations
derived from a Program Dependency Graph. On the Devign benchmark, VulGCL achieves
AUC\,=\,66.9\% and F1\,=\,65.3\%, outperforming all single-modality variants on
AUC. The ablation study confirms that graph structural and visual centrality
signals carry complementary information beyond semantic pre-training alone,
producing a consistent $+$1.07\,pp AUC gain over the strongest single branch.

Future work includes two-branch ablations to isolate the marginal contribution of
each modality pair, cross-dataset evaluation on BigVul to test generalization,
multi-seed training for statistical significance, a quantized edge variant
(VulGCL-Lite) for resource-constrained deployment, and integration of Stack
Overflow community signals as an additional modality.
